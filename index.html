Privacy Policy

Application: eBay Canada Automation V1
Application Owner: Muhammad Husnain
Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Overview

eBay Canada Automation V1 ("Application", "we", "our", or "us") is an e-commerce automation application operated by Muhammad Husnain. The Application is designed to assist authorized eBay sellers with seller-side product, inventory, listing, and related operational workflows.

The Application accesses eBay information only after an eBay user authorizes the Application through eBay's official authorization process.

2. Information We May Access

Depending on the permissions authorized by the eBay user and the functionality being used, the Application may access information made available through applicable eBay APIs, including:

  • Seller account and account-related information
  • Inventory and product information
  • Listing and offer information
  • Information relating to seller business policies where required for authorized operations
  • Order or fulfillment information only where the applicable functionality and authorization require it

The Application does not request or intentionally collect eBay account passwords.

3. How We Use eBay Information

eBay information is used only for authorized seller-facing functions of the Application and only to the extent reasonably necessary for those functions.

These functions may include:

  • Analyzing and managing seller inventory
  • Supporting product and listing workflows
  • Managing authorized listing and offer information
  • Supporting seller operational workflows and reporting
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, securing, and improving the Application's operation

We do not use eBay information for purposes that are not authorized by the applicable user or permitted under eBay's developer requirements.

4. AI-Assisted Processing

The Application may use authorized third-party software and artificial intelligence services to assist with permitted operational analysis, classification, drafting, or other seller-facing automation functions.

Where such processing is used, information is provided only to the extent necessary for the requested Application function and subject to applicable eBay requirements, privacy obligations, and service-provider controls.

We do not use eBay information obtained through eBay APIs to train, fine-tune, or improve third-party artificial intelligence models.

5. Google Sheets and n8n

The Application uses n8n-based automation workflows and Google Sheets for authorized operational processing, storage, organization, and workflow management.

Depending on the workflow, seller-related information obtained through authorized eBay access may be processed through these services to perform the requested seller operations.

6. Data Sharing and Service Providers

We do not sell eBay user information or other personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers that support the Application, including automation, cloud, spreadsheet, hosting, analytics, and permitted AI-processing services, only to the extent reasonably necessary to provide or secure the Application's functions.

We require such processing to be handled in accordance with applicable privacy, security, contractual, and eBay requirements.

7. User Authorization and Control

The Application accesses authorized eBay account information only after the eBay account holder completes eBay's authorization and consent process.

An authorized user may revoke the Application's access through the applicable eBay authorization or account controls. After revocation, we will stop accessing eBay information through that authorization, subject to legitimate retention requirements.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Personal information and eBay information are retained only for as long as necessary for the specific legitimate purpose for which they were processed, for applicable legal or security requirements, for authorized operational records, or for resolving disputes and maintaining the Application.

Information will be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer necessary for the applicable legitimate purpose, subject to applicable legal, security, audit, and retention requirements.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

API credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, passwords, and other authentication secrets are not intentionally published through this website or stored in publicly accessible source files.

10. Information Relating to Other eBay Users

Where the Application receives information relating to other eBay users in connection with authorized seller activities, such information will be processed only as necessary to facilitate permitted eBay services and in accordance with applicable eBay requirements, privacy obligations, and this Privacy Policy.

11. Compliance

The Application is intended to operate in accordance with applicable eBay developer requirements, the eBay API License Agreement, eBay's Privacy Notice, and applicable privacy and data-protection laws.

The Application does not knowingly use eBay services or eBay information for prohibited purposes, including the collection or storage of eBay user passwords or the training of third-party artificial intelligence models using eBay information.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application's functionality, data-processing practices, applicable laws, or eBay requirements change. The latest version will remain publicly available at this URL.
