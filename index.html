Privacy Policy

Application: eBay Canada Automation V1
Last Updated: September 29, 2026

1. Introduction

eBay Canada Automation V1 ("Application", "we", "our", or "us") is an automation application used by authorized eBay sellers to support e-commerce operations, including product and inventory management, listing workflows, offer management, order and fulfillment workflows, reporting, and related seller operations.

2. Information We Access

When an eBay user authorizes this Application through eBay's official authorization process, the Application may access information permitted by the eBay API scopes granted by that user.

This information may include, as applicable:

  • eBay account and seller information
  • Inventory and product information
  • Listings and offers
  • Order and fulfillment information
  • Seller business-policy information
  • Marketing or advertising information where separately authorized
  • Other eBay data necessary to perform the requested automation

3. How We Use Information

We use information obtained through eBay APIs only for the purposes authorized by the eBay account holder and necessary to provide the Application's functionality.

These purposes may include:

  • Managing and analyzing inventory and product data
  • Creating, updating, or managing eBay listing information
  • Managing offers and related seller workflows
  • Supporting order and fulfillment operations
  • Applying authorized seller business rules
  • Generating operational reports and analytics
  • Maintaining and troubleshooting the Application

4. Authorization and User Control

The Application does not access an eBay account on behalf of a user until the user completes eBay's authorization and consent process.

Users may revoke the Application's authorization through the applicable eBay account controls or authorization mechanisms. When authorization is revoked, we will stop using eBay data obtained through that authorization, subject to legal, security, audit, or operational retention requirements.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information obtained through the Application. We do not disclose eBay user information to third parties except when necessary to provide requested functionality, operate supporting services, comply with legal obligations, protect security, or as otherwise authorized by the applicable user.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the Application, such as hosting, automation, database, analytics, or software service providers, subject to appropriate contractual and security controls.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

Authentication credentials, API credentials, access tokens, refresh tokens, and other secrets are stored using secure credential-management mechanisms where supported and are not intentionally published on this website.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Application, maintain operational records, support security and troubleshooting, satisfy legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Personal information will be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer required for legitimate Application purposes, subject to applicable legal, security, audit, and technical retention requirements.

8. eBay Data and Compliance

The Application's collection, storage, use, and processing of eBay data are intended to comply with applicable eBay developer requirements, eBay's Privacy Notice, the eBay API License Agreement, and applicable privacy and data-protection laws.

eBay information is used only as necessary to facilitate the authorized eBay services and Application functions for which access was granted.

9. Other eBay Users

Where the Application receives information relating to eBay users other than the account holder who authorized the Application, such information will only be used to the extent necessary for permitted eBay-related activities and in accordance with applicable eBay requirements and privacy obligations.

10. No Password Collection

The Application does not request or intentionally collect eBay account passwords. eBay authentication is performed through eBay's official authorization process.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Application's functionality, data practices, legal requirements, or eBay requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL.
